==============================
COMPANY POLICY DOCUMENTATION
==============================

NOTE: This document is compiled from multiple policy versions (v1.2, v2.0, draft updates). Some sections may overlap or be partially outdated.

----------------------------------------
SECTION 1: GENERAL GUIDELINES
----------------------------------------

All employees, contractors, vendors must adhere to company policies.

Non-compliance may result in disciplinary action.

In certain cases, violations may not immediately result in termination but may lead to escalation.

---

----------------------------------------
SECTION 2: LEAVE POLICY (v2.0)
----------------------------------------

Employees are entitled to:
- 20 days annual leave
- 10 sick days

Unused leave can be carried forward for up to 6 months.

NOTE: Previous policy allowed 1 year carry forward.

---

SECTION 2A: LEAVE POLICY (OLD VERSION)
----------------------------------------

Employees were previously allowed:
- 18 days annual leave

Carry forward was allowed up to 12 months.

(This section may be outdated)

---

----------------------------------------
SECTION 3: REMOTE WORK POLICY
----------------------------------------

Employees may work remotely up to 2 days per week.

Manager approval is required.

Exception:
- Some teams allow full remote work depending on business needs.

Contractors:
- Typically not allowed remote access
- Exceptions must be approved by IT security

---

----------------------------------------
SECTION 4: IT SECURITY POLICY
----------------------------------------

4.1 SYSTEM ACCESS

Employees must not share credentials.

Password sharing is strictly prohibited.

---

4.2 SOFTWARE INSTALLATION

Unauthorized software installation is not allowed.

Consequences:
- Access revocation
- Warning or disciplinary action

NOTE:
Repeated violations MAY lead to termination.

---

4.3 DATA SECURITY

Sensitive data must not be:
- downloaded externally
- stored on personal devices

However:
Certain teams may use approved external tools.

---

----------------------------------------
SECTION 5: INCIDENT REPORTING
----------------------------------------

All incidents must be reported within 24 hours.

Failure to report:
- may result in warning
- in severe cases → disciplinary action

NOTE:
Some departments allow delayed reporting in exceptional situations.

---

----------------------------------------
SECTION 6: TERMINATION CONDITIONS
----------------------------------------

Termination may occur due to:

- repeated policy violations
- severe misconduct
- fraud or data misuse
- security breaches

---

----------------------------------------
SECTION 6A: TERMINATION (ADDITIONAL NOTES)
----------------------------------------

Not all violations lead to termination.

Examples:
- minor policy breaches → warning
- repeated violations → escalation

---

----------------------------------------
SECTION 7: FRAUD & COMPLIANCE
----------------------------------------

Fraud includes:
- misreporting financial data
- unauthorized transactions

Immediate termination in most cases.

However:
Final decision depends on investigation outcome.

---

----------------------------------------
SECTION 8: CONTRACTOR POLICY
----------------------------------------

Contractors:
- have limited system access
- must follow IT policies

Violation consequences:
- contract termination
- access revocation

---

----------------------------------------
SECTION 9: OPERATIONS POLICY
----------------------------------------

Employees are assigned shifts based on business needs.

Shift reassignment may happen without prior notice.

Repeated absence:
- warning
- possible disciplinary action

---

----------------------------------------
SECTION 10: DISCIPLINARY ACTIONS
----------------------------------------

Actions include:
- warning
- suspension
- termination

Decision factors:
- severity
- frequency
- business impact

---

----------------------------------------
SECTION 11: EXCEPTIONS & OVERRIDES
----------------------------------------

Certain policies may be overridden by:
- senior management
- compliance team
- legal requirements

These overrides may not be explicitly documented.

---

----------------------------------------
SECTION 12: AUDIT & MONITORING
----------------------------------------

All employee activity may be monitored.

Logs include:
- system access
- data usage
- application activity

---

END OF DOCUMENT
