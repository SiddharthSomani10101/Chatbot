COMPANY NAME: ABC CORPORATION
DOCUMENT: COMPREHENSIVE EMPLOYEE LEAVE POLICY
EFFECTIVE DATE: 01 JAN 2026

--------------------------------------------------

1. PURPOSE
This document establishes a standardized framework for employee leave entitlements, application procedures, approval workflows, and compliance requirements. The objective is to ensure fairness, transparency, and operational continuity while supporting employee well-being.

--------------------------------------------------

2. SCOPE
This policy applies to all full-time employees of ABC Corporation across all departments and locations. Contract employees may be governed by separate agreements.

--------------------------------------------------

3. DEFINITIONS
- Leave: Approved absence from work during scheduled working days.
- Manager: Direct reporting authority responsible for approving leave.
- HR System: Internal platform used for managing employee records and leave applications.

--------------------------------------------------

4. TYPES OF LEAVE

4.1 Annual Leave
Employees are entitled to 18 working days of paid annual leave per calendar year.
- Leave is credited on a pro-rata basis at the beginning of each month.
- Maximum carry-forward limit is 10 days into the next calendar year.
- Encashment of unused leave is subject to company policy.

4.2 Sick Leave
Employees are entitled to 10 days of paid sick leave annually.
- Medical documentation is mandatory for absences exceeding 2 consecutive days.
- Frequent sick leave patterns may be reviewed by HR.

4.3 Casual Leave
Employees are eligible for 7 days of casual leave annually.
- Intended for urgent or unforeseen personal matters.
- Cannot be combined with long-term leave unless approved.

4.4 Maternity Leave
Eligible female employees are entitled to 26 weeks of paid maternity leave.
- Additional unpaid leave may be granted upon request.

4.5 Paternity Leave
Male employees are entitled to 5 days of paid paternity leave.

4.6 Bereavement Leave
Employees may avail up to 3 days of leave in case of immediate family bereavement.

4.7 Unpaid Leave
Employees may apply for unpaid leave under exceptional circumstances.
- Approval is subject to business requirements and management discretion.

--------------------------------------------------

5. LEAVE APPLICATION PROCESS
- All leave requests must be submitted via the HR Management System.
- Employees must apply at least 3 days in advance for planned leave.
- Emergency leave must be communicated immediately.

--------------------------------------------------

6. LEAVE APPROVAL WORKFLOW
- Reporting managers review leave requests based on workload and staffing.
- HR may intervene in case of disputes or policy clarification.

--------------------------------------------------

7. HOLIDAYS
The company observes 10 public holidays annually.
A holiday calendar is published at the beginning of each year.

--------------------------------------------------

8. RECORD KEEPING
All leave records are maintained in the HR system.
Employees are responsible for regularly reviewing their leave balances.

--------------------------------------------------

9. POLICY VIOLATIONS
Misuse or falsification of leave information may result in disciplinary action, including termination.

--------------------------------------------------

10. AMENDMENTS
The company reserves the right to amend this policy at any time.

--------------------------------------------------

END OF DOCUMENT
